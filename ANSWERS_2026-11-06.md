## 1. The crew phone's total disagrees with the owner's — DIAGNOSED

**It is not a permissions problem and not a calculation problem.** I measured
what the server hands each login in your company, as that login, through RLS:

| | jobs | line items | runs | payments |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| postgres (everything) | 21 | 476 | 84 | 26 |
| Legacy Solutions, login 1 | 13 | 473 | 84 | 24 |
| Legacy Solutions, login 2 | 13 | 473 | 84 | 24 |
| Legacy Solutions, login 3 | 13 | 473 | 84 | 24 |

All three logins get **identical** rows. So both phones are entitled to the
same answer, and the difference is in what each phone has pulled down so far.

**The mechanism is documented in commit `0cf2ab7`.** On a fresh sign-in, the
phone first asks the server whether it may see money (`askMoneyScope()`). That
can answer **UNKNOWN** — not yes, not no, "could not tell". On UNKNOWN the job
sync does **no job work at all**: jobs, the catalog and the line items are all
skipped. It then retries three times (4s, 8s, 12s) and after that falls back to
a heartbeat — 60 seconds with the app open, **fifteen minutes in a pocket**.

Your 8,000 → 35,000 → 80,000 is that: each successful pass adds more line
items, and every total is recomputed from whatever has arrived. The totals are
computed on the phone from line items, runs and change orders, so a job whose
line items have not landed yet is simply a **cheaper job** — no error, no gap,
just a smaller number.

**What is actually wrong, and what I am fixing.** The retry bug itself was
fixed in 1.845, so the passes now recover. But the figure is still drawn as a
final number while the phone knows its data is incomplete: the "figures
settling" line only appears while the sync phase is `SYNCING`, and these retry
gaps sit in `OK` or `IDLE`. The app already has the state for this
(`MoneyScope.UNKNOWN`, `UnsyncedReason.ACCESS_NOT_CONFIRMED`) and the money
card already knows how to draw `--` instead of a number. It is simply not
wired up. A money figure must say "I cannot tell yet" rather than show a
confident wrong total.

**To check it after the next release:** sign out on the second phone, sign back
in, and watch the big "STILL OWED" block. It should read `--` with a settling
note until the phone has everything, then jump straight to the owner's figure.
It should never show an intermediate number as if it were final.

## 2. The deposit, and the drawing — YOUR RULE, AND A REAL BUG

**The formula, reinforced exactly as it stands in the code** (`JobMoney.ruleDeposit`,
and the identical copy in `supabase/functions/_shared/quote-deposit.ts`):

> **The deposit is the materials still to be bought, rounded UP to the next
> $100, plus another $100.**

- Capped at what is still owed on the job, so a deposit can never exceed the price.
- The extra $100 covers scheduling and transport. It is **never** itemised or
  explained on the quote page, the contract or the PDF — those print one
  deposit number. Your own screens may break it down; a customer-facing one
  must not.
- "Materials still to be bought" = material lines + tax on them + change-order
  material costs, **less** what has already been collected.
- Rounded UP means a round number stays put before the $100 is added: $1,000 of
  materials is an $1,100 deposit.

So **your rule — the deposit is always more than the material price — is
exactly what that $100 guarantees.** You are right, and the rule is in the code.

**THE BUG: re-pricing can break your rule.** When the price moves, a trigger
(`zz_deposit_follows_price`, applied yesterday) does **not** re-derive the
deposit from the materials. It scales the old deposit **proportionally**:

```
new deposit = old deposit ÷ old price × new price
```

Those are different numbers, and the proportional one can land **below the
materials cost** — which is you fronting material money, the exact thing your
rule exists to prevent. Worked example:

| | before | after a drawing change |
|---|---|---|
| materials | 1,000 | 2,000 |
| price | 5,000 | 8,000 |
| deposit by YOUR RULE | 1,100 | **2,100** |
| deposit the trigger stores | 1,100 | **1,760** ← below the 2,000 of materials |

So after adding fence to a drawing, the stored deposit can be $240 short of the
materials alone. The engine already has a warning for this state
(`warn_fronting_material`), which means the condition was anticipated but the
deposit was never made to follow the rule.

**What I propose, for your decision:** re-derive the deposit from the materials
rule whenever the price moves, instead of scaling it, keeping both of the
trigger's existing protections — it freezes the moment the customer is in it
(approved, signed, or any money landed), and a deposit you typed by hand in the
same save always wins. That makes the drawing change update the deposit by your
rule, immediately and server-side, so the estimate screen already shows the
right figure when you open it.

**The second half of your point — "immediately as I draw"** — is a separate
gap. Today the estimate screen shows the new figure as a *suggestion* behind a
"Set deposit" button; the stored deposit does not move until you tap it. With
the change above, the stored figure follows the drawing on its own and the
button becomes a correction, not a requirement.

## 9. "No utility locate" — what it means

`LocateTicket.State.NONE` meant **two different things at once**: "nobody has
called 811 yet" and "this job needs no digging". Because they were the same
value, the office's locate alert could never be satisfied for a job that
genuinely needs no locate — it fired **every day** such a job sat on the
schedule, and the only way out was muting the alert for every job at once.
That is how a safety alert dies.

They are now separate. `locate_not_required` is **somebody deciding**, with the
reason in the notes. The default is "nobody has said", which is the truth about
every job you already have, and the safe direction.

**Measured:** 13 jobs read as unanswered, of which only 2 are still ahead — so
the alert now shows **2 items instead of 13**.

**To check:** Office → a job → **HOA & Permits** → mark "no utility locate
required" with a reason. That job should drop out of the locate alert, and the
crew phone should stop warning about digging on it — the crew read the same
column, which `r58` added to their view, because otherwise a crew member keeps

## 10. "First sign in" — what it means

Signing in on a new phone was taking **up to fifteen minutes** to show your
work, and you reported it twice.

On sign-in the phone asks the server whether it may see money. If that answer
does not arrive, it tries again three times — 4s, 8s, 12s. The counter that
allowed those three retries was only ever reset by a pass that **got** an
answer. So three failed attempts disabled retrying **for the rest of the
app's life**, and every later pass fell back to a heartbeat: 60 seconds with
the app open, fifteen minutes in a pocket. One slow first connection and that
was the rest of your session.

It is now three retries **per sign-in**, refilled when the sign-in completes.

**To check:** on a spare phone, sign out and sign in on a weak connection. Your
jobs should appear within seconds, not minutes. Use `adb install -r` if you
want to keep the existing data on the device.
