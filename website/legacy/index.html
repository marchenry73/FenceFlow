A Legacy Solutions product

FenceFlow

FenceFlow is our specialised product for fencing contractors. It is built around how a fence job actually runs: measure it, price it, get it approved, build it, get paid.

Trace it, don't tape it

Build an estimate by tracing the fence on a satellite image or on a grid. Runs, corners and gates go on the drawing.

Material takeoff

The drawing produces the list: posts, panels, pickets, rails, concrete and gates. No separate spreadsheet to keep in step.

Works offline on site

No signal at the back of the property? Keep working. The job syncs later, when the phone is connected again.

Quotes customers can approve

Each quote is a web page the customer can open, review and approve, including a 3D view of the fence being proposed.

Crew time tracking

Crew members clock in and out, and their time is tracked against the work.

Card payments

Customers can pay by card, processed by Stripe.

Android app

For the field: measure, trace and build the estimate on site, with or without a connection.

Web office portal

For the office: manage jobs, quotes and crews from a browser.
