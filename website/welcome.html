Service agreement

Short, and worth reading. Typing your name signs it.

1. What FenceFlow is

FenceFlow is software for estimating and running fencing work. It is a tool you use; the fencing work, the quotes you send and the contracts you sign with your own customers remain entirely yours.

2. Your data is yours

Your jobs, customers, prices and photos belong to you. You can export them at any time, and nothing is deleted because a subscription lapses — if access ends, your records are still there and come back when it resumes.

3. Payment

Plans are billed monthly in advance and can be changed or cancelled at any time. Cancelling stops the next charge; it does not refund the current month.

4. Money you take from your customers

Card payments are handled by Stripe and go from your customer to Stripe to your bank. FenceFlow never holds your money and is not a party to those transactions.

5. What we don't promise

FenceFlow works out quantities and prices from what you tell it. Checking a quote before it goes to a customer is your responsibility, and the figures it produces are not a substitute for your own judgement.

6. Ending it

You can stop at any time. We may end an account for non-payment or misuse, and will always say which, in writing, before doing so.
