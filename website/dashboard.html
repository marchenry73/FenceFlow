Your alerts

The "Needs attention" list on Home can get noisy if you don't own a particular part of the job (scheduling materials, say). Turn off what you don't need — this only affects what you see; everyone else on your team still sees theirs.

Dismissed items stay dismissed until something about them actually changes. Desktop alerts pop up in this browser while this tab is open but not the one you're looking at — there's no email alert yet; the mail key isn't configured, and a silent failure to send would be worse than none.
