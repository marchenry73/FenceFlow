How a job runs

The same order you already work in.

1

Take the job

Customer details with address lookup. Photograph the survey or start on a scaled grid.

2

Draw the fence

Tap each corner. Drop gates anywhere. Mark the pool, easement, or trees the crew has to work around.

3

Get the numbers

Materials counted and priced. Adjust anything. Export a branded PDF and send it.

4

Run the install

Assign the crew, route the day's stops, text the customer you're on the way, work the checklist.

5

Get paid

Invoice, take card payment, ask for the review. See what the job actually made.
