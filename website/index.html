Why not the general one

Fence work is measured in feet, not in visits — and general job software has no idea what a linear foot is. Contractors who run fencing on generalist tools describe rebuilding the same post-and-panel arithmetic on every single quote, or paying to have the software customised into faking it.

How a job runs

The same order you already work in — nothing to re-learn.

Get paid
