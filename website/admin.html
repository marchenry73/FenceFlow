How suspension works

Stripe reports a failed payment and the company moves to past due. They keep working through the grace period — cutting a crew off mid-installation over a failed card punishes the customer standing in the yard, not the person who owes you. When the grace period runs out, Suspend stops the service. Suspension is always yours to press; nothing here suspends anyone automatically.
