FenceFlow

Privacy Policy

Last updated:

The short version: FenceFlow stores your fencing business's own data so you can run jobs. We do not sell your data, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not share it with anyone except the services listed below that are required to make the app work.

Who this covers

FenceFlow is used by fencing contractors. Two kinds of people appear in it: the contractor and their crew, who hold accounts, and the contractor's customers, whose details the contractor enters to quote and complete work.

What we collect

  • Account information — email address and password for anyone who signs in. Passwords are stored hashed by our authentication provider; we never see them.
  • Business information — company name, phone, email, licence number, pricing defaults, and message templates.
  • Job information entered by the contractor — customer name, address, phone, email, notes, fence measurements and drawings, materials, prices, schedules, payment amounts, and signatures.
  • Crew information — names, roles, contact details, pay rates, and clock in/out times.
  • Photos taken in the app of job sites and completed work.
  • Device information for notifications — a push token identifying the device, so job alerts can reach it.

We do not collect location tracking, contacts, browsing activity, or advertising identifiers.

Payment information

FenceFlow never receives, stores, or processes card numbers. When you take a card payment, the customer pays through your own payment provider (such as Square), and the money goes from them to your provider to your bank. We store only the amounts you record and a link to the payment page.

If you connect a payment provider, your access token is stored on your own device only and is never uploaded to our servers or shared with your crew.

How we use it

  • To run the features you use: estimating, scheduling, invoicing, reporting.
  • To sync your data between your own devices and your crew's devices.
  • To send notifications about jobs you or your crew are working on.
  • To provide support when you ask for it.

We do not use your data to train advertising models, and we do not sell it.

Who we share it with

  • Supabase — hosts the database and handles sign-in.
  • Google Firebase — delivers push notifications.
  • Your payment provider (for example Square), if you choose to connect one.

We share data with these providers only as needed to operate the service. We do not sell or rent your information to anyone, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.

Where your data lives, and for how long

Data is stored on Supabase's servers and on your own device. It stays until you delete it or close your account. Deleting a job removes it and its related records. Closing your account removes your company's data within 30 days.

Deleting your account and data

You can request deletion of your account and all associated business data at any time by emailing support@fenceflow.app from the address on your account. We will confirm and complete the deletion within 30 days. You may also delete individual jobs, customers, and crew records yourself at any time from the app or the office dashboard.

Your customers' information

If you are a contractor using FenceFlow, the customer details you enter are yours and you remain responsible for them. You should only enter information you are entitled to hold, and you should tell your customers how you use it. On request, we will help you export or delete any customer record.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Access is restricted at the database level so each business can only reach its own records, and crew accounts see less than owners and managers. No system is perfectly secure, and we will notify affected users promptly if we become aware of a breach involving personal data.

Children

FenceFlow is a business tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will update the date above and notify account holders by email before the change takes effect.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: support@fenceflow.app
