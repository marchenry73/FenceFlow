# FenceFlow: what to check by hand

Written 5 October 2026, after the office pricing redeploy, phone build 1.602 and the build after it.

**Ground rules**
- Use throwaway jobs named `TEST - delete me`, with your own email and phone, and delete them afterwards. Don't use "ZZ TEST" for a job: anything called that is deliberately never emailed, so the email checks would show nothing.
- On a real customer's job, only look. Don't press Approve, Pay, Send, Save, Re-price or Keep this price just to see what happens.
- A quote link that says "Sent ..., not opened yet." counts as opened the moment you visit it. Leave those alone.
- If a figure is off, even by a few dollars, stop. Note the job and the screen, tell Claude, and don't re-price or send that quote.

## Do these first

1. [ ] **Phone: is it the new build?** Settings, scroll to the bottom: "FenceFlow 1.NNN (build NNN)" should read build 660 or higher. If not, tap "Check for updates" and install before anything else.
   Why: 1.602 still has the two attach bugs and bills a stand-alone gate on the wrong post rules, so the drawing checks below would chase faults already fixed.

2. [ ] **Phone and office: does a sync lose materials?** Pick a live job with materials (Makayla's). In the office, Estimate & Pricing: note the Materials total, the number of lines and Contract. On the phone open the same job, press Sync Now and wait. Reload the office and reopen the job: all three unchanged.
   Why: on 1 Oct a sync marked 64 priced lines deleted in 19 seconds and left six jobs at labour only (about $34,000 of materials). It hasn't repeated in four days, which isn't proof.

3. [ ] **Office against phone: same price?** On three Draft or Sent jobs (one 6 ft vinyl, one with a gate), write down the phone's Materials, Labor, Gates, Tax, Subtotal and TOTAL (Go to Estimate). In the office open Jobs, the job, Estimate & Pricing, "Show full breakdown" (read-only) and compare.
   See: every pair matches to the cent (Labour = Labor, Gate charge = Gates, Grand total = TOTAL), totals with odd cents rather than round tens, six-foot posts on a six-foot job, and one gate post plus a latch post on a gate. On an Accepted job compare with the phone's TOTAL, not "Accepted price".
   Why: for two days the office used an old engine that rounded totals up to $10, priced the short post on a 6 ft fence and ignored the new gate rule, so its quotes came out low. It's redeployed, and this proves it. A note on minimum-charge jobs still says "rounded up ... to the next $10". That's out-of-date wording, not price.

On the phone make a quote PDF for any job and read the header at top left. To fix: empty the License number box, Save, open the phone app on wifi to sync, then make the PDF again.

8. [ ] **Phone: crew can't see money or delete.** Sign in on a second phone (or sign out and back in) as a crew login and open a job.
   See: the read-only "Fence Plan" ("Read only. If the line needs to move, ask - do not build it different."), no Estimate button and no dollar figure on the job, plan or run cards. After these, run the throwaway quote at the top of "Customer quote" below. It's the path your customers take.

- [ ] **"Still owed" is one number.** Compare Dashboard, Money, "Still owed", Reports, Business report, "Outstanding now", the total of the Outstanding column in Reports, Detailed tables, "Money owed", the four age boxes in Reports, Money, "Who owes you", and phone Home "Still owed to you". ### Quotes going out

- [ ] **A quote on unconfirmed prices won't go.** Find a Draft or Sent job whose "Needs attention" says "Priced on catalog prices nobody has confirmed: <name>". In "Customer quote page", "Copy link" and "Preview it" are greyed under a red "Not ready to send. N prices on this quote are still the ones FenceFlow shipped rather than yours...". - [ ] **"Re-price at the office" and "Keep this price".** Only on a job you'd re-price anyway (not James's or John's). See a green "Priced at the office" line with the figure and, below the total, "Priced at the office on <today>, engine 2026.10.10." - [ ] **"Send the quote again" only copies a link.** Open a job with the amber "Needs approval again" badge and press it. - [ ] **Your alert boxes are ticked.** Settings, "Your alerts": "A sent quote has gone quiet", "Approved with no deposit taken", "A payment attempt failed", "Money collected on a job later declined", "A payment dispute is open", "The drawing changed after approval". All ticked. Then Dashboard "Needs attention" should list "Approved, no deposit taken: <name>" for each approved job with a deposit set and $0 paid.
