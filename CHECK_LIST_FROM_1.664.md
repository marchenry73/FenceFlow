## Phone — the drawing screen

- [ ] **Long-press erases ONE wall, not the whole side.** An L-shaped side is one run with two walls, and long-press used to take both.
  - Draw an L: tap three points so there is a bend. Long-press the middle of ONE arm.
  - See: the dialog names that stretch. Confirm. The other arm stays. The bin in the tool bar still removes the whole side.

- [ ] **Erasing from the MIDDLE warns you the price goes up.** Two separate fences need four end posts where there were two, with their caps and concrete.
  - Draw one straight 96 ft side, then long-press the middle third of it.
  - See: a line saying it will leave TWO separate sides and the estimate gains two end posts, their caps and their concrete. On an END stretch that line is absent — it only shows when it applies.
  - After confirming, go To Estimate: the total should be **higher**, not lower. That is correct.

- [ ] **A gate on the erased stretch goes with it.** Shuffling it next door would silently re-price it against a different side.
  - See: a second line in the dialog saying the gate comes off too. Only when there is one.

- [ ] **Long-press does nothing in Adjust or Move View.** Holding still over a corner is how Adjust is meant to be used; the erase dialog used to interrupt it.
  - Tap **Adjust**, press and hold on a corner for two seconds.
  - See: no dialog. Only Draw offers erase.

- [ ] **The Adjust magnifier shows the fence.** It showed the photo and the markers and left out the thing you were adjusting.
  - Tap **Adjust**, press and drag a corner.
  - See: inside the round loupe, the fence line and the corner you are holding.

- [ ] **Double-tap to finish a side leaves no stray point.** The tap that opened the dialog used to stay on the drawing — two coincident points, which is what gave one gate four posts.
  - Draw two points, then double-tap to finish.
  - See: the bar's corner count is what you drew, not one more.

- [ ] **A gate can stand on its own.** A gate tapped in open ground used to be swallowed by whichever side was selected, however far away.
  - On the job with the 96 ft side, tap **Gate**, then tap in **open ground** well clear of the fence. Set Feet 4, **In the fence line**, Add Gate.
  - See: a new side of its own holding just that gate. To Estimate → **2 blank posts, the gate and the hardware. No end posts, no panels.**
  - Then tap **Gate** and tap ON the 96 ft line: that gate joins the fence, even if another side is selected.

- [ ] **Curved fence.** Three taps: where it starts, a point it must run **through**, where it ends.
  - Tap the **Curve** segment. Place three taps in a bow.
  - See: a dashed preview after the third tap, then an ordinary side you can nudge in Adjust.
  - To Estimate: **Corner posts 0** (or unchanged). A curve must not quietly buy corner posts — they are a different catalog row at a different price.
  - Leave the mode with only two taps down: the half-placed curve is discarded, not attached to your next tap.

- [ ] **Gate hanging on the fence, latching to the wall.** New option, its own entry — not a reword of an existing one.
  - Add a gate and choose **Latches to a wall**.
  - See: **one** post, hole plugs for the wall side, one lot of concrete. Not two posts.

- [ ] **Live quantities while you draw.** The bar updates as the drawing changes rather than only on To Estimate.

- [ ] **Estimated hours follow the drawing.** They followed the footage on the job screen only, so a side drawn here left them frozen until you next opened the job.
  - Note the job's **Est. duration**, draw another 50 ft, come back.
  - See: the hours have moved. If you ever **typed** an hours figure yourself, it must NOT move — that is deliberate.
